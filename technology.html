TECHNOLOGY

Careful science behind every step.

Fertility treatment depends on a well-run laboratory. Here is how technology supports your care at Nius We Care.

Embryology Laboratory

Eggs, sperm and embryos are handled in a controlled laboratory environment with stable temperature, air quality and gas conditions, supported by trained embryologists.

Embryo Culture & Freezing

Embryos may be cultured to the blastocyst stage when appropriate, and vitrification (fast freezing) is used to preserve eggs and embryos for future use.

Genetic Testing (PGT)

When medically indicated, preimplantation genetic testing can assess embryos for specific genetic or chromosomal conditions, alongside counselling.

Safety & Witnessing

Careful labelling, double-checking and documentation at each step help ensure the right samples are matched to the right patients.
