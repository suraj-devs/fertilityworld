Understanding Genetic Testing

Preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) assesses embryos for particular genetic or chromosomal conditions when medically indicated. Testing is discussed alongside your fertility treatment, family history and genetic counselling.

Is this right for you?

Testing may be discussed for known inherited conditions or selected cases of recurrent pregnancy loss and unsuccessful treatment. It is not necessary for everyone and cannot guarantee pregnancy or a child without all health conditions.

What your care may include

  • Review of medical and family history
  • Discussion of PGT-A or PGT-M where indicated
  • Genetic counselling and informed consent
  • Embryo testing coordination
  • Results discussion and individualized next steps

This information is educational and does not replace an individual medical assessment. Procedures, timelines and outcomes vary. Your doctor will explain the benefits, limitations and risks before you decide.

Every journey starts with questions. Here are a few we hear often, answered with care.

When should I see a fertility specialist?

Generally, seek evaluation after 12 months of trying if you are under 35, or after 6 months if you are 35 or older. Seek advice earlier for irregular periods, known fertility concerns, recurrent pregnancy loss or other relevant medical history.

Is IVF the only option for infertility?

No. Depending on the cause, your specialist may discuss ovulation treatment, IUI, surgery in selected cases, IVF or other options. Both partners should be evaluated before deciding on a treatment plan.

How much does IVF treatment cost in Jaipur?

Your total cost depends on medicines, investigations, the treatment protocol and any additional procedures or storage. Request an individualized written estimate from Nius We Care that clearly explains what is included and excluded.

How long does an IVF cycle take?

An IVF cycle often takes several weeks, but your schedule can vary with the protocol, test results and whether a fresh or frozen transfer is planned. Your specialist will explain your expected timeline.

Can IVF guarantee a successful pregnancy?

No fertility treatment can guarantee pregnancy. Outcomes vary with age, egg and sperm quality, medical history and other factors. Ask your specialist for an individualized, realistic discussion of success rates.
